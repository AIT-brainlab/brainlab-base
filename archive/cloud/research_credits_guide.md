# How to Get Free Google Cloud Funding & Research Credits: AIT Brainlab Guide

This guide explains how every researcher, PhD student, postdoc, and faculty member at **AIT Brainlab** can apply for and receive direct cloud funding and research credits from Google to power their GCP workloads.

---

## 1. Google Cloud Research Credits Program

Google provides direct financial credits for academic research projects conducted at universities like AIT.

> [!NOTE]
> **Master's Students & Research Assistants**: While Master's students cannot apply individually, you can work with your advisor / PI (e.g. Brainlab PIs) to apply for a **Faculty Research Credit** of up to $5,000 USD that covers your project.

## 2. TPU Research Cloud (TRC) Program (Free TPUs for AI/ML)

If your research involves **deep learning, LLM fine-tuning, computer vision, or compute-heavy ML models**, apply for the TPU Research Cloud:

- **Official Website**: [TPU Research Cloud (TRC)](https://sites.research.google/trc/)
- **What You Get**: Free access to Cloud TPU clusters (TPU v2, v3, v4, v5e) on GCP.
- **Requirement**: Agree to share research findings via open-source code, blog posts, or peer-reviewed academic publications acknowledging TRC support.
- **Approval Time**: Fast turnaround (usually 1–2 weeks).

## 3. Google Cloud Teaching Credits (For Course Instructors)

If you are a faculty member or teaching assistant running a course at AIT:
- **Official Website**: [Google Cloud Teaching Credits](https://edu.google.com/programs/teaching-credits/)
- **What You Get**: **$50 to $100 USD** in GCP credits per enrolled student for hands-on assignments.

## 4. Step-by-Step Application Walkthrough for Brainlab Members

Follow these 4 steps to apply for your credits:

### Step 1: Get Your Academic Email & Billing Account Ready
- Ensure you have an active academic email (`@ait.asia` or `@ait.ac.th`).
- Ensure you have an upgraded GCP Billing Account ID ready (see [`cloud/README.md`](README.md)).

### Step 2: Calculate Your Budget
- Open the [Google Cloud Pricing Calculator](https://cloud.google.com/products/calculator).
- Estimate the cost of compute (e.g., Spot VMs, GPUs), storage (GCS), and data processing (BigQuery) for your research duration (e.g. 6 to 12 months).
- Export or save the calculation link/summary.

### Step 3: Submit the Application
- Go to the [Google Cloud for Researchers Application Portal](https://edu.google.com/programs/research-credits/).
- Fill in:
  - **Academic Institution**: Asian Institute of Technology (AIT)
  - **Department**: Computer Science and Information Management (CSIM) / DPI Center
  - **Project Abstract**: Concise summary of research problem, methodology, and expected impact.
  - **GCP Services Used**: Specify Compute Engine, Vertex AI, BigQuery, or GCS.
  - **Pricing Calculator Link**: Paste your estimated budget link.

### Step 4: Redeem & Link Credits to Brainlab Projects
- Once approved (takes ~6–8 weeks for Research Credits, ~1–2 weeks for TRC), Google sends a promo code email.
- Go to **GCP Console** $\rightarrow$ **Billing** $\rightarrow$ **Credits** $\rightarrow$ click **Redeem**.
- Link the credits to your research project (e.g. `brainlab-res-[your-name]`).
- Enable budget alert safeguards (50%, 80%, 90%, 100%) so out-of-pocket charges never occur.
